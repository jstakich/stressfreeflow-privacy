Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

Overview

Stress Free Flow is a relaxation-focused application designed to help users reduce stress through calming visuals, interactive elements, and soothing ambient sounds . The app provides a peaceful space to relax, focus, and unwind.

Mission & Development

This app is a native Swift project built by a developer with 20 years of experience. It was specifically designed as a sensory-friendly tool for the developer's non-verbal great nephew, Ollie, who has autism. It prioritizes soft motion, haptic feedback, and background audio utility without ads or subscriptions.

No data collection

Stress Free Flow is built on a foundation of trust and privacy. This app does not collect, store, track, or share any personal data or user information. You can use all features of the app without creating an account or providing any personal details.

Stress Free Flow does not use analytics, tracking tools, or third-party services that collect user data. Your experience stays private and focused on your well-being.

All features of Stress Free Flow are self-contained within the app. No user data is transmitted to external servers for app functionality.

App features and functionality

The app includes a feature that lets you share videos with a QR code. The code links to the App Store so others can find the app; it does not process external payments.

The Gentle Reminder feature reminds you to return to the app for relaxation. It uses standard local notifications when the app is not in use. No personal information is collected or transmitted for this feature.

Background audio you start in the app may continue while the device screen is locked or while you use other apps. See Background audio below. This does not involve background data collection or tracking.

Background audio

Stress Free Flow can keep one relaxing background sound playing continuously while your device is locked, so you can rest or use it as a sound machine. Playback is entirely under your control.

1st way to start sound machine: On the app’s Home screen, under Choose a Background Sound, select a sound other than Off and set the volume. Lock your device. The sound keeps playing. On the Lock Screen, use the standard Now Playing controls for this app to pause, resume, adjust volume, or switch sounds when available.

2nd way to start sound machine: Open Settings in the app, then Audio Options / Vibration / Background Sound. Turn on one background sound and set its volume. Lock your device. The sound keeps playing. Use Now Playing on the Lock Screen to pause, resume, adjust volume, or change the sound.

Your sound may continue while you stay in the app, return to the device Home screen, switch to another app, or leave the phone locked. To stop playback, choose Off for the background sound on the app’s Home screen, or turn off the selected sound in Audio Options / Vibration / Background Sound.

This functionality is user-controlled and does not involve data collection, tracking, or external communication by the app for that purpose.
